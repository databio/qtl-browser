## The GTEx collection

The dataset as served is the eQTL Catalogue's uniform reprocessing of GTEx v8 (its own pipeline,
its own SuSiE credible sets), so it has two publications: the data's and the processing's.

- `citation`: the GTEx Consortium, "The GTEx Consortium atlas of genetic regulatory effects across
  human tissues", Science 369:1318–1330, 2020, doi 10.1126/science.aaz1776.
- `source`: eQTL Catalogue release 6, study QTS000015, datasets QTD000256 and QTD000260; Kerimov
  et al., "A compendium of uniformly processed human gene expression and splicing quantitative
  trait loci", Nature Genetics 53:1290–1299, 2021, doi 10.1038/s41588-021-00924-w.

Both DOIs resolve on Crossref to these titles, volumes and pages, and the entry is in
`config.yaml` `collections:`. `data/raw/papers/kerimov2021_eqtlcatalogue_natgenet.pdf` is the
Nature open-access PDF; the GTEx PDF would not download to curl (Europe PMC and eScholarship both
serve a bot page) and is still to fetch by hand. The `source` dict is free-form, so the
Catalogue's paper sits in `source` beside the record without a schema change; the general app's
About page shows both.

## What this changes elsewhere

- **R4 (b)** puts ~8 GB on this machine's `data/derived/` beside the 4.2 GB local store, and the
  upload sends it all again (the 53 shared objects aside). Nothing on Rivanna changes.
- **R5** means the TOPCHeF app's `VITE_COLLECTION_OF=topchef_ge_cis` keeps working unchanged
  against the combined store; it ignores the GTEx experiments. The general app's bundle grows by
  the chooser and shrinks by the coloc table and the speculative-pointer code.
- **R2** deletes `ui/src/lib/coloc.ts`'s pinned loci from the general app, so its Home has no
  colocalization track unless a collection carries one; today none does.
- **R3** adds a second variant catalog and the `gencode_v39` annotation to the upload; the bench
  and smoke suites stay TOPCHeF-only.
- **R8**: between the store upload and the UI deploys, both live apps show their version error
  instead of the spinner they show today.
- **Credentials**: R4 (a) would copy the B2 key to Rivanna; R4 (b) does not.
